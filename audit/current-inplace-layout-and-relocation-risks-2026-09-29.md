# Current in-place layout and relocation risk audit (2026-09-29)

All measurements below use the current 705-object strict MSVC x86 `/O1 /GS-` set and the pinned original ASI (`409F0DF7AE579841DB05C3EC6DAD0A9AFC579194632874962E1BDEE0CCF020D3`). These are feasibility inventories, not a PE patch or a production-loadability claim.

## Entry placement

- `audit/entry-trampoline-feasibility-reportfault-context-asm-linked-2026-09-29.json`: **705/705** entry symbols resolve to executable diagnostic-link addresses; 147 entries need a 5-byte `E9 rel32` thunk, all displacements are in range. This demonstrates address/reach feasibility only.
- `audit/inplace-text-packing-reportfault-context-asm-linked-2026-09-29.json`: the heuristic can park 145 oversized bodies in existing `.text` gaps; two cannot fit in any remaining contiguous gap. They are `1001eb27` (2,640-byte candidate body, 1,756-byte entry gap) and `1001f203` (4,108-byte body, 2,296-byte gap). Although 22,825 total free bytes remain, the largest contiguous gap after the heuristic placements is 2,333 bytes.
- A separate `/O2` size-only probe did not solve the two cases: `1001eb27` emitted a 2,656-byte entry body and `1001f203` 3,890 bytes. They still exceed their original gaps. These objects are probes, not replacements or semantic validation.
- A read-only PE header check (`audit/pe-appended-code-section-capacity-2026-09-29.json`) found 344 bytes of header slack (eight 40-byte section-header slots), no overlay after the last raw section, and room to append one executable section. The two currently unplaced entry bodies total 6,748 bytes; a provisional append could start at RVA `0x43000` / raw offset `0x3c600`, use at least 7,168 aligned raw bytes, and raise estimated `SizeOfImage` to `0x45000`. This only indicates header/file capacity: it does not verify a PE edit, loader behavior, or account for additional helper COMDATs and relocation records.
- Follow-up sizing uses the actual 147 thunk-required bodies (not just the two bodies that cannot fit any simulated free gap): `audit/appended-thunk-payload-census-2026-09-29.json`, generated by `scripts/audit-appended-thunk-payload.py` from the fresh strict COFF objects. At provisional RVA `0x43000`, deterministic 16-byte alignment gives **48,232 body bytes / 49,412 payload bytes**, requiring **49,664 raw bytes** at 512-byte file alignment and estimated `SizeOfImage=0x50000`. Those 147 bodies contain **676 DIR32** and **536 REL32** COFF relocations. This is the more relevant append-section alternative after rejecting the unsafe gap placements; it is still only sizing and relocation inventory. Every REL32 must be recalculated against its final destination and symbol; the DIR32 targets and new base-relocation blocks must be resolved; the 18 original HIGHLOW records intersecting the five-byte entry-thunk windows must be reconciled; then all original PE directories, startup, and runtime behavior must be validated. No image builder currently performs those steps.
- A symbol-index-aware pass (`scripts/audit-appended-thunk-relocation-targets.py`; result `audit/appended-thunk-relocation-targets-v2-2026-09-29.json`) inspected all 1,212 fixups in the 147 current COFF bodies and the definitions in all 705 candidate objects. It resolves **459 REL32 and 7 DIR32** records directly to candidate roots, **4 REL32** records to a helper defined in another candidate object, and **74** records to same-object sections (52 DIR32 to `.rdata`; 16 DIR32 and 6 REL32 to local `.xcode` symbols). The remaining **668 undefined-symbol records** need final link/address resolution: 601 DIR32 occurrences span 338 names (173 `DAT`/`PTR`-pattern symbols, 45 import-slot names, 120 other names), and 67 REL32 occurrences span 30 names. The local `.rdata`/`.xcode` targets mean copying only each root body is insufficient; companion constant/helper sections must be placed or safely redirected. The unresolved symbols are not automatically linker errors—the diagnostic link may resolve them—but their addresses must be mapped to the final PE image. No relocation has been applied to a final image.
- The expanded current-object report `audit/appended-thunk-relocation-targets-v4-2026-09-29.json` inventories the companion sections too: the 52 `.rdata` references cover **47 section contributions in 14 candidate objects**, totaling **726 raw bytes**. One companion `.rdata` section has a `DIR32` at offset `0x18` to candidate root `0x1001072a`; copying this read-only data therefore adds a further absolute pointer that must be fixed up and included in the PE base-relocation directory. The 16 local `.xcode` DIR32 and six REL32 targets lie in root body sections already counted in the 48,232-byte payload.
- The exact-name diagnostic-map join (`audit/appended-thunk-link-map-resolution-v4-2026-09-29.json`) finds all **668/668** undefined-symbol fixups in the successful diagnostic link map. Of these, 384 occurrences (173 names encoding original `DAT_`/`PTR_DAT_` VAs) point to provider addresses displaced by the diagnostic layout; a final in-place image must map them to the encoded original VAs, not copy the diagnostic addresses. Another 72 occurrences across 45 `__imp_*` names are import slots; `audit/appended-thunk-original-import-crosswalk-v2-2026-09-29.json` matches all **45/45** by API and DLL to the pinned original PE's IAT and records their exact original slot VAs. Remaining targets include 175 code-symbol occurrences (134 names, including runtime/RenderWare/address providers) and 37 provider/runtime-data occurrences (15 names); their final preferred-base destinations and support-code placement still need explicit reconciliation. These results demonstrate diagnostic-link resolution and a useful target map, not a production-ready relocation pass.
- A first relocation-directory constructor is now implemented and self-checked: `scripts/build-appended-relocation-directory-probe.py` emits `build/pe-layout-probe/appended-reloc-directory-147-only-v2-20260929.bin`, summarized by `audit/appended-reloc-directory-147-only-v2-2026-09-29.json`. It independently parses the original PE directory: **4,681** original HIGHLOW sites total (3,160 `.text`, 1,247 `.rdata`, 274 `.data`), removes the 18 sites intersecting the 147 five-byte entry-thunk windows, adds 677 DIR32 fields for the appended code plus its referenced `.rdata`, and round-trips the resulting **5,340-site** table exactly. The 11,212-byte encoded directory fits in the existing `.reloc` section (16,196 virtual / 16,384 raw bytes), so no second relocation section is needed for this placement. Payload geometry is `.xcode` RVA `0x43000`, raw `0x3c600`, 50,199 bytes before file alignment, 50,688 aligned raw bytes, estimated `SizeOfImage=0x50000`.
- This is still only the relocation table for the **147 thunk-only entries**. It does not reconcile the 557 candidate bodies placed in their original gaps, apply any code/data COFF fixups, include all support providers, alter PE headers/directories, or pass loader/GTA tests; the standalone `.bin` is not an ASI.
- The first overlap run used an entry-slot-only xref CSV and was too narrow to validate moved-body destinations. The full-range Ghidra export is `ghidra_exports/text-nonentry-reference-audit-20260929.csv`; applying it to the same 145-body packing gives `audit/inplace-text-reference-overlap-full-range-reportfault-context-asm-linked-2026-09-29.json`: **207 unique target addresses and 255 reference rows overlap 21 proposed placements**. Joining destination bytes classifies the unique targets as **190 instruction bytes and 17 data bytes** (220 InstructionDB and 35 DataDB reference contexts); 50 conditional and 6 unconditional jump references occur in those raw records. These overlaps mean the heuristic is **not cleared for use**, but do not alone prove every edge stays live: current candidates replace many source functions, and some sources are old switch tables/unowned tails. A source-reachability classification is still required before accepting or rejecting those placements semantically. The earlier entry-slot-only zero-overlap result does not clear this blocker.
- Follow-up source-owner checks now identify persistent startup-data conflicts among the references from outside candidate entry slots; the 145-body packing must be **rejected as currently proposed**, not merely left undecided. In particular, proposed placement of `10018cf7` at `100208c7` overwrites the startup callback entries `100208d0..10020a50` addressed by `.rdata` pointers `1002215c..100221ac`; the callback-array crosswalk in `audit/crt-init-array-source-crosswalk-2026-09-27.csv` maps these to real candidate startup functions (including `10020a80` and `100211b0`). Proposed placement of `1001a900` at `10013025` also collides with initializer code addressed by `.rdata` slots `100221c0` and `100221dc` (`audit/asi-text-highlow-unmatched-instruction-windows-2026-09-27.csv` and `audit/crt-init-array-entries-ghidra-2026-09-27.csv`). These references cannot be dismissed as switch-table bytes owned by replaced bodies. Separately, moving `10019eb3` to `1001ae2a` covers the `__cfltcvt` fallback code pointer at `1001af2b`; CRT startup normally rewrites the ten entries before the plugin DllMain, but this does not prove there is no earlier CRT use (`audit/asi-data-relocation-target-classification-2026-09-27.md`). The `10013a10` target is `__local_unwind4` handler code referenced from a SEH registration path and data slot, so it too needs explicit preservation. These classifications do not prove every one of the 255 raw xrefs live; they are sufficient to reject these particular destinations. A separate appended executable section or a newly computed placement avoiding every persistent target is needed.
- A separate all-entry overwrite-window audit uses actual clobber extents (full candidate body for direct placements, exactly 5 bytes for thunk entries). Current strict sizes yield **18 Ghidra reference rows / 16 unique target VAs** inside five direct-body spans. Source/function context adjudicates these as old switch-table cells whose dispatchers are replaced (`100060d0`, `100154dc`), two self references removed with `10009790`, and two residual branches in old thunked bodies (`10018f6c`, `1001cb20`) cut off by entry thunks. The `1001c5e0` pointer is outside the corrected 40-byte `1001c5b8` body. This covers recorded Ghidra references, not undiscovered indirect/dynamic references. Current geometry report: `audit/inplace-entry-reference-byte-windows-final-current-2026-09-29.json`; exact-body follow-up: `audit/1001c5b8-and-1001cb37-exact-inplace-followup-2026-09-29.md`.

## Fixup work still required

- The thunk-specific HIGHLOW audit was refined to select only the **147** entries that currently require thunks: `audit/entry-thunk-relocation-overlap-planned-only-reportfault-context-asm-linked-2026-09-29.json` finds 18 original `.text` HIGHLOW DWORDs intersecting their patch windows (2 wholly overwritten, 16 partially overwritten).
- Among the 557 bodies currently fitting at their original entries, `audit/inplace-base-relocation-reconciliation-reportfault-context-asm-linked-2026-09-29.json` finds 1,873 original HIGHLOW sites intersecting body spans, 1,708 candidate DIR32 fields, 220 exact site matches, 874 shifted partial-overlap pairs, and 44 original HIGHLOW fields straddling candidate-body boundaries. It also records 820 old fields with no candidate DIR32 intersection and 662 candidate DIR32 fields with no old HIGHLOW intersection. Each class needs instruction/target adjudication before constructing the final relocation directory.
- Candidate code/data targets, original IAT slots, shifted bodies, the two appended bodies, and PE section/header growth are not yet integrated into one verified image builder. The diagnostic DLL remains non-loadable as ImVehFt.

The candidate C++ remains useful reverse-engineering output, but unadjudicated source liveness for capacity-only placements, the two oversized bodies, and unresolved base-relocation reconciliation prevent claiming all 705 are ready for a production-compatible ASI. The latest exact-body follow-up changed `1001c5b8.cpp` and `1001cb37.cpp` with backups; earlier layout probes did not modify candidate source.
