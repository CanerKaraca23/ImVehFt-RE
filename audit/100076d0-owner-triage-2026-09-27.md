## Effect on the residual and what this does not prove

These 19 owners are removed from the syntactic false-positive set, reducing the
conservative defined-owner residual from 43 to 24; the already-resolved
ownerless indexed store remains excluded. This is a static provenance
classification only. It does **not** identify the actual runtime value of
`RwGlobals.stdFunc[5]`, prove all indirect/reentrant paths, or justify turning
the `100076d0` ReAgent parity result green. That target remains YELLOW pending
the live slot writer/value and all-path callback/re-entry invariant.
